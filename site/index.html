Meet Dr Costantino Davide

Dr Davide believes beauty comes from balance and harmonious proportion. He listens first, then decides on the right path together with you.

He qualified in medicine and surgery at the University of Trieste and spent more than a decade in general and vascular surgery at the city's university hospital. He then specialised in plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery at the University of Padua, and completed postgraduate training in Rio de Janeiro under Professor Ivo Pitanguy, one of the most influential plastic surgeons of the twentieth century.

Since then he has operated in Italy, Albania, Togo and Dubai, where he led a plastic surgery department. He has practised in London since 2018 and continues to see patients in Trieste.
