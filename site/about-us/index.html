Beauty is a question of balance, not excess

Aesthetic surgery is not only about changing one area of the face or body. It is about the harmony of the whole person.

The aim is to improve what causes you discomfort or dissatisfaction while keeping your features coherent, natural and in proportion. Every request is assessed carefully, and solutions that are excessive or wrong for you are never proposed.

Whether you are considering face, breast or body surgery, the process always begins with an open conversation about your wishes, your limits, what is possible and the result you can realistically expect.

What you can expect from us

Choosing a surgeon means trusting technical skill, an aesthetic eye, a serious method and an honest relationship. This is how each of those shows up in your care.

A personal approach

Treatment is only suggested after a careful look at your anatomy, your goals and whether it is truly right for you.

Natural results

The aim is to improve the harmony of your face and body without artificial or excessive change. Friends should notice you look well, not that you have had surgery.

The whole picture

Your assessment considers both the aesthetic result and your overall wellbeing, so the path chosen is the right one for you.

Care before and after

From your first visit to recovery, you are guided with clear, precise instructions and follow-up for a year or more.

Your safety comes first

Surgery deserves time, attention and accurate information. Every step is planned so that you feel informed and at ease.

  • One or two consultations so you never decide in a hurry.
  • Anaesthesia discussed openly. You meet the anaesthetist and know exactly what to expect.
  • Fully equipped surgical facilities in London and Trieste.
  • Follow-up for a year or more after your operation.

Two cities, one standard of care

See Dr Davide in central London or in Trieste, in English or Italian. Wherever you start, your plan, your surgeon and your aftercare stay the same.
