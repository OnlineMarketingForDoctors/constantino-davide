A surgeon who listens first

Dr Davide believes beauty comes from balance and harmonious proportion. He takes time to understand what you would like to change, then decides on the right path together with you.

He qualified in medicine and surgery at the University of Trieste and spent more than a decade in general and vascular surgery at the city's university hospital. That grounding in major surgery still shapes the precision and safety of his work today.

He went on to specialise in plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery at the University of Padua, then completed postgraduate training in Rio de Janeiro under Professor Ivo Pitanguy, one of the most influential plastic surgeons of the twentieth century.

Since then he has operated in Italy, Albania, Togo and Dubai, where he led a plastic surgery department. He has practised in London since 2018 and continues to see patients in Trieste.

Proportion over perfection

For Dr Davide, a good result is one nobody can point to. He will tell you honestly when a request would look out of proportion, and plan every change in relation to the rest of your face or body.

The Pitanguy school

Professor Ivo Pitanguy trained generations of plastic surgeons from around the world in Rio de Janeiro. His teaching put harmony and the patient's wellbeing at the centre of aesthetic surgery.

Dr Davide is a member of AEXPI, the association of Professor Pitanguy's former students, and brings that same philosophy to every consultation.
